The problem with keyword screening

ATS systems filter on keywords. Candidates have learned to game them. The result? You miss great people and waste time on keyword stuffers.

What ATS sees

A candidate lists "C#, .NET, Azure, HL7, FHIR, healthcare, SQL Server" and 40 other technologies. Eight years of experience. Keywords match the job description perfectly. ATS ranks them highly.

Reality? Zero quantifiable achievements. No evidence of depth. No metrics, no impact, no architectural decisions. Just a skills list.

What Signum Vero sees

A candidate from a different tech stack led a microservices migration maintaining 99.97% uptime, implemented PCI-DSS compliance, and is actively learning your stack through personal projects.

Most ATS systems would reject them outright. Signum Vero flags them as a strong architectural fit with transferable regulated-industry experience.
